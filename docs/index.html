Motion sources: what is available here?

HumanoidMimicGen: a 25.7-second G1 demonstration pushing a full-height wheeled cart by 2.39 m. The NPZ includes the original cart mesh, wheel and payload motion, and floor. Arm poses are adapted for spherical pads. This is recorded simulation motion, not motion capture or data from the heavy-pushing paper. Dynamic tracking and payload forces are unvalidated. The source dataset card does not specify a data license; the cart assets retain their MIT notice. Source and reproduction ↗

HOI-Retarget: four G1 pushing references from OMOMO: box, table, plastic box, and suitcase. Videos replay the exported ball-hand G1 and the supplied moving object mesh. NPZs include the floor, object trajectory, geometry, and source contact annotations. Motion data: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; object meshes: MIT. Source and reproduction ↗

OmniRetarget: all 29 released climbing clips, with supplied terrain. These come from a source cited by ACP; its exact clip selection is not public.

LAFAN: the importer is available, but its five terrain-free previews were removed from this gallery.

Mixamo: five ladder clips plus seven reviewed box-climb, crouch/duck, and gap-jump candidates. Each video shows the terrain stored in its downloadable NPZ. The ladder references are restored with their existing fitted terrain and quality reports. “Jump Up On Box” and the other excluded clips remain removed. Support contacts and dynamic tracking remain unvalidated. Excluded clips and reasons ↗ Source and reproduction ↗
